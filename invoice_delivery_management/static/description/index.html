Invoice Delivery Management

Designed to save time by linking inventory directly to invoices.

When an invoice is confirmed, the quantity of the product or products on the invoice is deducted from stock automatically.

How it works

Three steps — and only the first one is yours.

1. Confirm the invoice

Create a customer invoice as usual and click Confirm. Each line shows the available quantity, in red when stock is not enough.

2. Delivery is created

A delivery order is generated from the invoice warehouse, hard-linked to the invoice, with units of measure converted automatically.

3. Stock is deducted

The delivery is validated at once and on-hand quantities drop immediately. The invoice chatter logs the transfer.

Key features

Automatic stock deduction

Confirming a customer invoice deducts its storable and consumable products. Services are ignored.

Credit notes return stock

Confirming a customer credit note puts the products back in stock (can be switched off per company or per credit note).

Reverse on reset / cancel

Reset to draft or cancel reverses the stock movement (lots included). Re-confirm and the new quantities are deducted — stock always matches the invoice.

Shortage policy

When stock is insufficient: allow negative stock, keep the delivery waiting, or block the invoice confirmation with a clear message.

Multi-company & multi-warehouse

Warehouse per invoice, defaulting from company settings or the user's warehouse. Each company keeps its own options.

No double deduction

Invoice lines coming from a Sales Order are skipped — the order delivers them. Manual lines on the same invoice are still deducted.

Protected invoices

Only Accounting Managers can delete, cancel or reset to draft an invoice whose delivery is completed.

Arabic included

Full Arabic translation of fields, settings, messages and errors.

Configuration

  1. Install the module (depends on Invoicing and Inventory).
  2. Go to Invoicing › Configuration › Settings › Invoice Stock Deduction.
  3. Optionally pick the default warehouse and the shortage policy; every option is company-specific.
  4. Confirm a customer invoice — done.
